Methodological Note for Literary Scholars, Critics & Digital Humanities

Why a Reference Avant-Garde Corpus is Essential: Traditional stylometric systems evaluate poetry either by rigid syllabic-accentual scansion (penalizing free verse and dolnik) or by commercial readability scores (penalizing metaphor and syntactic inversion). 1. The Moth
Original: «Мотылёк» • Ufa, Russia
Gorging on wet asphalt, Slick with rain-foam. Rearing up, I scrape my skin, Tear it, Swallow it mile by mile. Left and right — a jungle of lines And shadows. A tunnel... Down the center — a concrete steppe. Streetlamps, Like the Illuminati of a Cathedral, Smeared by headlights, By velocity... A flash. A butterfly — two wings Locked in the crosshairs of the pupil. Screech — brakes — Eyes.... Instants... Dimensions fracture — Of flight, Of a hurtling moth Beating against the lamp of the Universe — Its streetlight...
Stylometric Profile: High-velocity syncopation (Metric #8), harsh consonantal clusters ("screech — brakes", Metric #3 & #4), and abrupt conceptual scaling (Metric #10).
2. The Tea Bag
Original: «Пакетик чая»
I compute a galaxy of quanta In the droplets upon your hair. I refresh the streams of data Inside my interface eyes. With a neuron I brush reality, Embodied in a tea bag, Clinging to the wall of a glass, A dried neutron at the edge. We lie in a single bed, Sketched out along our desires. With our lips we have pierced the stars Of incarnated minds. By the strings of our embrace The universe shimmering in tea is transfixed, A neutrino frozen for a second... Countdown zero — one!
Stylometric Profile: Signature Concept Rhyme (Metric #10) colliding quantum physics with domestic detail (94/100 Ostranenie Index).
3. The Sculptor
Original: «Ваятель»
No, I am likely not a poet — Across the span of lived-out years I do not rhyme my lines to please, Or soothe the ear with sugary verse. I break the meter like a fool — No, I am likely not a poet. There is no chamber music in my speech, No pretty, polished phrase — I never tame the world into a stream. No, I am likely not a poet, More like a butcher or a sculptor. I slice away, I rip the pamphlet, And every mimicry of "writer." I hack my speech like rawest meat, Like a sculptor — chiseling off the excess, Leaving the naked nerve exposed. No, I am likely not a poet — Once baring not the nerve, the bone, I bared before the crowd Not myself — the living Soul. And with a cleaver, chisel, hand, I carve the Muse out of raw image.
Stylometric Profile: Anti-ornamental poetics manifesto aligned with the UFLI critical tradition. High verb-to-adjective energy and deliberate metrical disruption (Metric #7).
4. The Display Color of Her Eyes
Original: «Дисплейный цвет её глаз»
I wrenched open the ribs of my desires, Baring with raw emotion The muscle of my heart, Ripping the scab Off bandaged memories of pain. Your palm presses your lips — Mine, To yours; Through parched pipes flow Resinous feelings — in words Sticky with betrayal, Into an open chest — Like rains Exhausted by change. With crooked fingers I clutch your wrist, Tormented By the agony of rejection: "I won't be poisoned again, Inoculated by the immunity of knowledge." You forgive me in the past; In the present, you drink wine. I hear you no longer — I left you long ago. The display color Of your sparking eyes, Smelling of the anesthesia of complex happiness, Held us together with a surgical stitch. We are in one bed again, Sharing a single pillow for two. My palm upon your body Traces along your veins algorithms of curves Inside a space of jagged system objects.
Stylometric Profile: Psychopoetic tension combining visceral physicality ("wrenched", "ribs", "surgical stitch") with cybernetic metaphor ("anesthesia of complex happiness", "algorithms of curves").
5. The Nomad
Original: «Кочевник» • Ufa, Bashkortostan
Conquista down Salavat Avenue — A frantic rhythm Hammering the heart, Trance, Music of headlights Rushing toward The Nomad. A steed — a steel ideal, Hooves tearing the asphalt's soul, Roar of the engine Ripping my own soul apart. Vast expanses of Ufa, Outer space, Steed of the wind — freedom of the steppes.
Stylometric Profile: Horizontal alliteration and acoustic resonance ("steed — steel ideal", "tearing", "roar", "ripping", Metric #1 & #2) fusing Eurasian steppe heritage with urban velocity.
6. The Möbius Car
Original: «Машина Мёбиуса»
I got into the car Amidst skyscrapers And lively courtyards. I painted a picture: Here a lovely girl Walked behind her lover; There a hooligan dog, A headstrong cat. I watched it all as an artist, A friend, a comrade, A poet, a sculptor, A husband and a lover, Simply living On this planet — Perhaps a writer Without awards or epithets. I got into the car And drove, hurtling Along the parapets, Amidst skyscrapers, Past the lovely girl Who now, arm in arm, Walked again with her lover; Past the hooligan dog And the headstrong cat. I breathed freedom, Painting the picture Where I got into the car again And admired it all as a sculptor, Being husband, poet, and writer.
Stylometric Profile: Distant semantic recursion (Metric #9) forming a closed topological loop of urban observation and artistic detachment.
7. The Glitch
Original: «Глюк»
Suddenly washed away Into the Brownian sludge Of a concrete megacity mix, Hung up In thought On the wires, With the intellect of a paramecium, Wedged Into frames Of drywall, cardboard, office, Plastic, White, toxic LED lamps; Like a tablet's plug I dangle Joylessly — A crash, A glitch Born inside a system of "Friend-or-Foe": Desk, trash bin, laptop, Stapler — My brain convulsively claws At the washed-out reality Of rain In a fading megacity Seeing no — EXIT.
Stylometric Profile: Telegraphic accentual verse (Metric #6) with sharp line-break syncopations culminating in an abrupt semantic cutoff.
